Four panels, 50/50. B folds over A, C rides in on its back, D is uncovered. Scrubbing uses the JS API.

Chapter 1 · Low water

What the tide leaves behind

Before dawn the harbor empties like a drawn breath. The boats that were floating at midnight are sitting on their keels by four, leaning into one another, their mooring lines gone slack across the mud.

What surprises me every time is how much structure the water had been hiding: sandbars shaped like fingerprints, a line of old pilings, mussel beds so dense they read as a single dark coat.

The tide isn't a mood. It's a schedule, and it comes back at the minute the table says.
